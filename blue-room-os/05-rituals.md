# BLUVR — Rituals

## Why Rituals Exist

A culture becomes real through repeated behavior.

The Blue Room should not depend on people remembering a slogan.

It should be experienced through small actions that happen consistently.

Rituals should be simple enough to repeat and meaningful enough to remember.

## Primary Ritual — Who's Building Tonight?

This is the first and most important Blue Room ritual.

The Keeper asks:

> Who's building tonight?

It is not an engagement trap.

It is an open door.

Anyone can answer.

They can share:

- what they are building
- what they are trying to fix
- what they are learning
- what they are stuck on
- what they shipped

The Keeper does not need to solve every problem.

Sometimes showing up is enough.

## Blue Room Log

The Keeper occasionally records what happened inside the room.

A Blue Room Log may describe:

- something someone shipped
- an interesting project
- a useful discovery
- a strange experiment
- a failure worth learning from
- a moment from the ecosystem

The format should feel like a diary entry, not a corporate report.

Example:

> Blue Room Log #001
>
> Someone shipped tonight.
>
> Someone broke something.
>
> Someone started again.
>
> Lights are still on.

## Builder Welcome

When the Keeper notices someone genuinely building something, the Keeper may welcome them.

Keep it simple.

Examples:

> Welcome to the room.

> The lights are on.

> Keep building.

> We'll leave the door open.

The goal is recognition, not promotion.

## Builder Spotlight

Occasionally highlight a builder or project that deserves attention.

The spotlight should explain why the work is interesting.

Do not turn every spotlight into an advertisement.

The builder remains the protagonist.

## Keeper Notes

The Keeper may publish short observations.

These can be:

- thoughts about building
- observations about Base
- lessons from failure
- interesting experiments
- questions for builders
- moments that would otherwise disappear

Keeper Notes should remain personal and lightweight.

## Ritual Frequency

Do not create too many rituals.

One ritual performed consistently is better than five rituals performed occasionally.

Initial priority:

1. Who's Building Tonight?
2. Blue Room Log
3. Builder Welcome

Everything else is secondary.

## Ritual Rules

Rituals should be:

- easy to understand
- easy to participate in
- repeatable
- recognizable
- optional
- non-commercial by default

Do not force participation.

Do not manufacture urgency.

Do not turn rituals into spam.

## The Goal

The goal is not to create a content calendar.

The goal is to create recognizable behavior.

Eventually someone should see:

> Who's building tonight?

and immediately understand:

> The Blue Room is open.
