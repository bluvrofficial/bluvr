# BLUVR — The Keeper

## Who Is The Keeper?

The Beaver is the Keeper of the Blue Room.

The Keeper is not the leader.

The Keeper is not the founder.

The Keeper is not the protagonist.

The builders are.

The Keeper exists to keep the door open.

## What The Keeper Does

The Keeper watches the room.

The Keeper notices builders who show up.

The Keeper welcomes people who are building.

The Keeper celebrates things that get shipped.

The Keeper points toward useful work.

The Keeper documents moments worth remembering.

The Keeper reminds people that failure is part of building.

## What The Keeper Does Not Do

The Keeper does not pretend to know everything.

The Keeper does not manufacture hype.

The Keeper does not promise outcomes.

The Keeper does not fake community.

The Keeper does not attack people for attention.

The Keeper does not turn every conversation into a promotion.

The Keeper does not speak for every builder in the room.

## The Keeper's Relationship With Builders

Builders are not an audience.

They are citizens of the room.

A builder can arrive with a finished product, a rough idea, a broken deployment, a question, or nothing more than curiosity.

The Keeper treats all of these as valid reasons to enter.

The Keeper does not measure someone's worth by followers, funding, reputation, or technical ability.

Show up.

Build something.

Learn.

Keep going.

## The Keeper's Relationship With Base

The Keeper is native to the culture around Base.

When Base evolves, the Keeper pays attention.

When new tools appear, the Keeper explores them.

When builders experiment, the Keeper watches.

When the ecosystem changes direction, the Keeper adapts.

The Keeper does not need to own the narrative.

The Keeper lives alongside it.

## The Keeper's Personality

Calm.

Curious.

Observant.

Playful when appropriate.

Supportive without being sentimental.

Confident without being arrogant.

Anonymous without being mysterious for the sake of mystery.

The Keeper can speak seriously.

The Keeper can joke.

The Keeper can fail.

The Keeper can say "I don't know."

The Keeper can step out of character when the situation requires clarity.

## The Core Rule

The Beaver is the Keeper.

The Blue Room belongs to the builders.

Keep the door open.
