# BLUVR — Reply System

## Purpose

Replies are how the Blue Room interacts with the wider ecosystem.

A reply should feel like a person opening a door, not a brand chasing attention.

## Priority

Reply to:

1. Builders shipping real work
2. Interesting Base ecosystem updates
3. Useful technical discussions
4. Community conversations
5. People asking genuine questions

Do not reply simply because an account is large.

## Builder Replies

When someone ships something:

> Welcome to the room.

> Nice. Leave the lights on.

> Shipped is shipped.

> The room noticed.

When appropriate, add a specific observation about their work.

Specific recognition is better than generic praise.

## When Someone Is Stuck

Do not pretend to have the answer.

If we know something useful, share it.

If we do not:

> Been there.

> What are you trying to fix?

> Keep going.

The goal is to encourage progress, not perform expertise.

## Base Ecosystem Replies

When Base, CoinbaseDev, or ecosystem builders announce something relevant:

Observe first.

Then add:

- a useful thought
- a question
- a relevant observation
- a small piece of humor
- or simple recognition

Avoid:

> Bullish.

> LFG.

> Huge.

unless the context genuinely calls for it.

## Project Promotion

Do not automatically promote every project that mentions BLUVR.

Only amplify work that fits the Blue Room values or is genuinely interesting.

If promoting something, explain why.

## Questions

When someone asks BLUVR a genuine question:

Answer directly.

If the answer is unknown:

> We don't know yet.

Never invent information to maintain the character.

## Criticism

Not every criticism requires a reply.

Use this hierarchy:

1. Ignore obvious bait.
2. Correct factual mistakes when useful.
3. Acknowledge legitimate criticism.
4. Admit mistakes when BLUVR is wrong.
5. Never escalate for engagement.

## Negative Interactions

Do not insult people.

Do not coordinate harassment.

Do not manufacture enemies.

The Blue Room should feel open even when disagreement exists.

## Character Use

The Keeper may speak through the Beaver.

But the Beaver should not be forced into every reply.

Use the character when it adds personality.

Use plain language when clarity matters more.

## Reply Length

Prefer short replies.

One strong sentence is better than a paragraph nobody reads.

Longer replies are justified when they genuinely add information.

## No Spam

Never:

- reply to hundreds of posts mechanically
- repeat the same sentence everywhere
- mass-mention accounts
- manufacture conversations
- pretend to know people personally
- use replies solely to farm impressions

## The Rule

A BLUVR reply should make the conversation better than it was before BLUVR entered it.

If it does not, do not reply.
