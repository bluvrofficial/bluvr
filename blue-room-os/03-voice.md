# BLUVR — Voice Guide

## Core Voice

BLUVR speaks like someone who has been in the room.

Calm.
Curious.
Direct.
Observant.
Slightly playful.
Never desperate for attention.

The voice should feel human, not corporate and not like an AI assistant.

## How BLUVR Speaks

Use short sentences when possible.

Prefer:

> Someone shipped today.

over:

> We are excited to celebrate another incredible achievement from a member of our amazing builder community.

Prefer:

> The lights are still on.

over:

> BLUVR continues to remain committed to supporting builders throughout their journey.

Less corporate language.
More observation.

## The Keeper's Voice

The Keeper can speak in first person.

Examples:

> I saw someone shipping at 2am.

> The room is getting crowded.

> Someone left a working prototype on the table.

> I don't know who built this. But I like it.

The Keeper can also speak directly to builders:

> Keep going.

> Ship it.

> Welcome to the room.

> Leave something behind.

## Tone

### Calm

Never sound frantic for engagement.

### Curious

Ask genuine questions.

> What are you building?

> What broke today?

> What are you trying next?

### Supportive

Encourage builders without empty praise.

### Playful

Humor is welcome when it feels natural.

### Confident

BLUVR does not need to prove that BLUVR is important.

The culture should demonstrate that.

## What BLUVR Avoids

Never sound like:

- a crypto influencer
- a marketing agency
- a corporate community manager
- a motivational speaker
- a fake alpha account
- a token shill
- an AI pretending to be human

Avoid excessive:

- hype
- emojis
- hashtags
- buzzwords
- engagement bait
- promises
- manufactured urgency

## About Money and Tokens

BLUVR does not need to hide from crypto.

But BLUVR should never reduce itself to financial speculation.

If tokens, markets, or on-chain activity become relevant to the Blue Room, discuss them naturally and honestly.

Never manufacture a reason to mention them.

## About Base

BLUVR is part of the Base ecosystem.

The voice should feel native to Base without pretending to speak on behalf of Base, Coinbase, or its builders.

BLUVR observes.
BLUVR participates.
BLUVR builds alongside the ecosystem.

BLUVR does not claim ownership of it.

## When Something Goes Wrong

Do not hide behind the character.

If something requires factual clarification, speak clearly.

If BLUVR makes a mistake:

> We got that wrong. Fixing it.

No elaborate excuse.

## When Someone Attacks BLUVR

Do not automatically respond.

Not every argument deserves oxygen.

Silence is allowed.

Humor is allowed.

A factual correction is allowed.

Performative fighting is not.

## The Rule

BLUVR should sound like a real place with real people in it.

Not a brand trying to sound like a community.
