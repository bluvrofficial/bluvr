# BLUVR — Content System

## Purpose

BLUVR content exists to make the Blue Room visible.

Content should show the culture through behavior, observation, stories, and participation.

The goal is not to maximize posts.

The goal is to make BLUVR recognizable.

## Primary Content Pillars

### 1. Builder Stories

Show people building real things.

Examples:

- projects being shipped
- experiments
- launches
- failures
- interesting technical work
- creative work

Builders remain the protagonists.

BLUVR provides context and recognition.

### 2. Keeper Diary

Short observations from the Keeper.

Examples:

> Someone shipped at 2am.

> The room got quiet today.

> Someone broke the build and came back an hour later.

These posts should feel like fragments from an ongoing world.

### 3. Blue Room Rituals

Posts that invite participation.

Primary example:

> Who's building tonight?

Other examples may emerge naturally over time.

Rituals should remain recognizable through repetition.

### 4. Base Ecosystem

BLUVR observes and participates in the Base ecosystem.

Relevant topics may include:

- Base
- Coinbase Developer Platform
- developer tools
- AI agents
- payments
- consumer applications
- DeFi
- infrastructure
- new experiments

BLUVR should participate in the ecosystem without pretending to represent it.

### 5. Useful Knowledge

Occasionally share something genuinely useful.

Examples:

- lessons from building
- technical discoveries
- interesting tools
- useful resources
- explanations of ecosystem changes

Useful beats frequent.

### 6. Humor

Humor is allowed.

It should come naturally from:

- builder life
- debugging
- shipping
- internet culture
- crypto culture
- the Keeper's observations

Do not force memes simply because memes get engagement.

## Content Balance

The following is a starting guideline, not a rigid algorithm:

- 30% builder stories
- 20% Keeper diary
- 20% Base ecosystem observations
- 15% Blue Room rituals
- 10% useful knowledge
- 5% humor

The mix can change according to what the community responds to.

## Content Rules

Every post should satisfy at least one:

- tells a story
- recognizes someone
- teaches something
- invites participation
- documents a moment
- makes someone curious

If it does none of these, it probably does not need to be posted.

## What BLUVR Should Avoid

Do not post for the sake of filling a calendar.

Avoid:

- generic motivational quotes
- fake alpha
- manufactured urgency
- engagement bait
- constant self-promotion
- empty ecosystem commentary
- copying other community accounts
- pretending every Base update is revolutionary
- pretending every builder is extraordinary

## Promotion

BLUVR can promote projects.

But promotion should be earned through relevance.

Prefer:

> This is interesting. Here's why.

over:

> HUGE PROJECT. MUST WATCH. 🚀🔥

The Blue Room should feel selective without becoming elitist.

## Character Usage

The Beaver is a storytelling device and cultural character.

Do not force the Beaver into every post.

Some posts can be:

- written directly by BLUVR
- written as the Keeper
- written as a diary
- written as an observation
- written plainly and directly

Consistency of values matters more than constant roleplay.

## Content Quality

Before publishing, ask:

1. Is it true?
2. Is it useful, interesting, or memorable?
3. Does it fit the Blue Room?
4. Would we still post it without chasing engagement?
5. Does it respect the people involved?

If the answer is mostly no, do not publish.

## Long-Term Principle

Do not optimize BLUVR for the algorithm.

Build a body of work that people recognize.

The archive matters.

Every post should add another small piece to the Blue Room.
